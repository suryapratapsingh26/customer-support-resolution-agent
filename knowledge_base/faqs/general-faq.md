# Synthetic demonstration policy: general customer support FAQs

This synthetic FAQ applies to the fictional Northstar Electronics store. It is demonstration content, not a real company commitment.

## Getting help

Support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time, excluding public holidays. Include the order number and a short description when contacting support. Never include passwords, one-time passcodes, full payment-card numbers, or security codes in a message.

## Orders, products, and account details

Order status and tracking are available from the order page. Product specifications on the product listing describe the item's supported features; if a specification is missing, ask support to confirm it before purchase. Customers can request an account email update after verifying account ownership. Support will not ask for an account password or one-time passcode.
