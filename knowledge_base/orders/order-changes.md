# Synthetic demonstration policy: order cancellation and changes

This synthetic policy applies to the fictional Northstar Electronics store. It is demonstration content, not a real company commitment.

## Cancellation

An order may be cancelled while its status is processing and before a shipping label is handed to the carrier. Once dispatched, it cannot be cancelled; the customer may request a return under the refund eligibility policy. Cancellation requests are not complete until the order page confirms cancellation. Approved cancellations are refunded to the original payment method, usually posting within 5–10 business days after approval.

## Address and item changes

Contact support promptly to request an address or item correction. Changes are possible only before fulfilment begins and are not guaranteed. Support must verify the order and confirm whether the change succeeded; customers should not assume a carrier redirect is available.
