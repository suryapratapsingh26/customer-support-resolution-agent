# Synthetic demonstration policy: payment failures and duplicate charges

This synthetic policy applies to the fictional Northstar Electronics store. It is demonstration content, not a real company commitment.

## Failed payments

If checkout reports a failed payment, first check the order page before trying again. A pending authorization is not a completed payment and normally disappears within 3–7 business days, depending on the bank. If the order was not created and the authorization remains after seven business days, contact support with the date, amount, and payment method. Never send a full card number or security code.

## Duplicate payments

If two completed charges appear for one order, retain both transaction references and contact support. Support verifies the payment processor records before initiating a refund. Confirmed duplicate captures are refunded to the original payment method; issuer posting commonly takes 5–10 business days after approval. Do not promise a refund for an authorization that has not settled.
