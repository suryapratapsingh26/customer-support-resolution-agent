# Synthetic demonstration policy: refund eligibility and timelines

This synthetic policy applies to the fictional Northstar Electronics store. It is demonstration content, not a real company commitment.

## Eligibility and timing

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days after delivery for an unused item in its original packaging. Faulty or incorrectly supplied items may be reported within 90 calendar days after delivery. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Banks and card issuers usually post the refund within 5–10 business days after approval; the store cannot control the issuer's posting time.

## Partial and full refunds

A full refund covers the eligible amount paid for the returned item, including its allocated standard shipping charge when the entire order is returned. A partial refund may be issued for a verified partial return, a missing item, or an agreed price adjustment. The total of all refunds for a payment must never exceed the amount captured. Promotional discounts are allocated proportionally across items.
