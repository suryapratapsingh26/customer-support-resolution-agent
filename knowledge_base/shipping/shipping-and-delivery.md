# Synthetic demonstration policy: shipping delays and delivery estimates

This synthetic policy applies to the fictional Northstar Electronics store. It is demonstration content, not a real company commitment.

## Estimated delivery and delays

The delivery date shown at checkout is an estimate, not a guarantee. Standard domestic delivery is usually 3–7 business days after dispatch. Tracking may take up to 48 hours to show its first carrier scan. Severe weather, remote destinations, and carrier capacity can extend transit. When tracking has no update for 3 business days beyond the estimate, support should check with the carrier and give the customer the next confirmed update.

## Lost or damaged shipments

If tracking says delivered but the parcel cannot be found, check household members, the building reception, and the carrier's delivery photo, then contact support within 7 days. Report visible transit damage within 48 hours of delivery and include photographs of the item and packaging. Keep the item and packaging until support confirms whether inspection or return is needed. Support investigates with the carrier before offering a replacement or refund; do not promise either before review.
